Our Story

Sunshine Bakery was born in a small Accra kitchen in 2009, fuelled by a grandmother's sourdough starter and an obsession with the perfect golden crust. Every loaf, roll, and pastry we bake carries the warmth of that original flame — slow-fermented, hand-shaped, and honest about its ingredients.Our methods follow the principles championed by the Sourdough School ,where slow fermentation is both science and art.

Today our ovens run before dawn so you wake up to bread that is still breathing. We believe a great bakery belongs to its neighbourhood — which is why we source locally, hire locally, and give away yesterday's loaves every evening to those who need them most.

Meet the Founder

Abena Mensah learned to bake at age seven, standing on a stool beside her grandmother in Cape Coast. After a decade working in hotel kitchens across Europe, she returned to Ghana with one goal: to bring the patience and craft of old-world baking home. Sunshine Bakery is that homecoming.
